# FieldGuard Brand Exploration

*Proposal only: this exploration does not rename or modify the current CropSentinel application.*

## Positioning

FieldGuard is a calm, evidence-aware field-observation companion that helps growers and agricultural students turn pest photos into understandable next steps.

## Name exploration

- **FieldGuard** — Suggests attentive field stewardship and practical protection without promising a diagnosis or a treatment.
- **CropLens** — Clearly connects crops and observation, but feels more like an imaging tool than a learning and decision-support product.
- **Fieldwise** — Communicates practical judgment, but is less distinctive and does not immediately evoke field observation.

FieldGuard is preferred because it balances trust, care, and memorability while leaving room for education, observation history, and cautious guidance. “Guard” should describe the product’s supportive role, not imply guaranteed protection.

## Brand voice

FieldGuard is:

- **Trustworthy:** distinguish observations, model output, and verified guidance.
- **Practical:** give clear, useful next steps in plain language.
- **Calm:** avoid alarmist wording and make uncertainty easy to understand.
- **Evidence-aware:** state limitations, show confidence as an indicator rather than proof, and encourage confirmation from a qualified local source.

Use short sentences, specific verbs, and respectful language for growers and students. Avoid absolute claims such as “guaranteed,” “always,” or “cures.”

## Light-mode color palette

| Role | Color | Use | Contrast intent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forest | `#1F5132` | Primary headings, navigation, and primary actions | Dark enough for white text and strong text contrast |
| Leaf | `#3F7D4A` | Secondary actions and positive accents | Use with white text only after contrast verification |
| Sunlit cream | `#F7F5ED` | Page background | Keeps the interface warm and low-glare |
| Paper | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and form surfaces | Provides a clear surface against the cream background |
| Soil | `#3A3028` | Body text | Intended for high-contrast text on light surfaces |
| Sky | `#D9EEF2` | Informational panels | Pair with Soil text, not white text |
| Amber | `#A86400` | Caution labels and uncertainty cues | Pair with white or Paper only when the final contrast check passes |
| Alert red | `#A33A32` | Errors and safety-critical notices | Pair with white text and an icon or label, never color alone |

Text and controls should target WCAG AA contrast (at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text or UI graphics). Statuses must use text or icons in addition to color.

## Typography direction

Use **Source Sans 3** for interface text and **Source Serif 4** for occasional educational emphasis; both are open fonts with broad language support. If they cannot be loaded, fall back to the web-safe stacks `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` and `Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`. Keep body text at a comfortable reading size and use weight and spacing, rather than all caps, for hierarchy.

## Logo and icon direction

The logo can combine a simple shield outline with a single leaf or field row, using an open shape that remains legible at small sizes. Avoid literal insects, pesticide containers, or imagery that suggests guaranteed pest removal. Icons should be rounded, two-dimensional line forms with consistent stroke weight: photo, field, magnifier, observation history, and information. Use original geometric forms rather than tracing or imitating copyrighted artwork.

## UI headline options

1. Observe the field with confidence.
2. Turn a photo into a clearer next step.
3. Learn what to look for in the field.
4. Evidence for the next field walk.
5. Start with an observation.

## Safety-aware empty states and errors

1. **No observation yet.** Add a clear field photo to begin; results are educational clues, not a confirmed diagnosis.
2. **We could not read that image.** Try a brighter, closer photo with the subject in focus.
3. **No close match found.** Record the field clues and confirm the finding with a qualified local source.
4. **History is empty.** Your saved observations will appear here; avoid uploading personal or identifying information.
5. **Guidance is unavailable.** Continue with non-chemical inspection and follow authoritative local advice before taking action.
